## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-velocity growth, digital precision, and authoritative marketing intelligence. Rooted in the visual identity of performance marketing, it projects momentum, technical mastery, and commercial elevation. The target audience comprises venture-backed founders, CMOs, and enterprise marketing executives seeking high-ROI scale and disciplined performance analytics.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modern** rigor with **High-Contrast Kinetic** accents. The structural foundation relies on deep, obsidian navy tones (`#0A0E1A`) contrasted against crisp polar neutrals (`#F7F8FB`), allowing the primary incendiary apex orange (`#FF6B2C`) and catalytic amber gold (`#F59E0B`) to command focal attention with uncompromising energy. Interfaces are deliberate, ultra-legible, and metric-focused—eschewing frivolous ornamentation in favor of purposeful information hierarchy, tight typographic rhythm, and performance-led visual drive.

## Colors

The color palette leverages functional polarization to maintain extreme clarity while generating marketing impact:

- **Primary (`#FF6B2C`)**: An incendiary, energetic orange representing apex velocity, action, and key conversion moments. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, key performance highlights, and directional focal indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0A0E1A`)**: A deep, commanding obsidian blue-black providing grounding structural contrast, high-authority typography, and dark-mode anchor surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: A vibrant marketing amber used for tactical accents, micro-badges, status highlights, and secondary graphical vectors.
- **Neutral (`#5A6578`)**: A balanced slate-gray anchor that calibrates mid-level text, icon states, subtle division lines, and muted metadata.

### Application Guidelines
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The default canvas uses `#F7F8FB` to soften glare while maintaining optical brightness. Interactive cards and panels sit on pristine `#FFFFFF`. Dark modules utilize `#0A0E1A` with elevated layers at `#141A29`.
- **Text Contrast**: Text on light backgrounds defaults to `#0A0E1A` for WCAG AAA compliance. Secondary labels leverage `#64748B`. Any text overlay on the primary orange must strictly use `#FFFFFF` with bold weights to maintain legibility.
- **Data & Micro-Accents**: Reserve tertiary `#F59E0B` for secondary milestones, trend indicators, and auxiliary alerts without competing against the `#FF6B2C` conversion pathway.

## Typography

The typographic strategy synthesizes geometric, technical impact with clear, welcoming readability:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an authoritative, tech-forward cadence suited for conversion metrics, impactful value propositions, and commanding marketing narratives. Tight negative letter-spacing ensures a solid visual presence at scale.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers crisp geometric clarity, humanist warmth, and exceptional legibility across dense dashboard cards, analytics tables, and descriptive narrative sections.
- **Hierarchy Scaling**: Hero headlines compress cleanly across mobile viewports via responsive tokens (`display-mobile`, `headline-lg-mobile`), preventing awkward hyphenation and preserving vertical scanning momentum.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `1280px`. Spacing and micro-alignment conform to an 8-point base spatial rhythm, supplemented by 4-point micro-steps (`space-2xs` at 4px) for tight component internals.

### Breakpoints & Spatial Distribution
- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Deep section padding scales up to `space-3xl` (`72px`) for dramatic macro rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins. Multi-column cards shift from 3-up to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Grid tracks reflow to a unified single column; micro-spacing tightens to `space-xs` and `space-sm` to maximize vertical viewport economy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and diffused ambient shadows rather than stark elevation:

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: Surfaces sit on canvas neutral `#F7F8FB` with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels)**: Raised surfaces on `#FFFFFF` are encased in a subtle boundary stroke (`1px solid #E2E6EF`) combined with a soft, warm-tinted ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Navigation & Hover States)**: Dynamic hover transforms lift cards `2px` along the Y-axis, transitioning to an intensified shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Dialogs)**: Deep focus elevation featuring `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.18)` overlaying an obsidian backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
- **Primary Glow**: Buttons and conversion-critical elements in focused or active states emit an accent ambient bloom: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(255, 107, 44, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape geometry (`roundedness: 2`):
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, text fields, badges) utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radiuses.
- Content containers, cards, and modal dialogs scale up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to frame data sets gracefully.
- Metric indicators, status tags, and pill chips utilize full pill radiuses (`9999px`) to visually separate qualitative tags from rigid data cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#FF6B2C` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `Space Grotesk` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold label. Height: `44px` (desktop), padding: `0 20px`. Hover initiates a slight brightness lift with a primary ambient bloom (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(255, 107, 44, 0.35)`). Active scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: Solid `#0A0E1A` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for secondary high-authority commands.
- **Outline**: `1.5px` border in `#E2E6EF`, transparent background, `#0A0E1A` text. Hover shifts background to `#F7F8FB` and border to `#0A0E1A`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, `#0A0E1A` text, hover reveals a `#F7F8FB` background fill.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed in an `8px` rounded box with `#FFFFFF` background and a crisp `1px` border in `#E2E6EF`.
- Typography is `body-md` (`16px`) to eliminate iOS input zoom.
- Focus state shifts the border to `#0A0E1A` with a subtle focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.08)`. Error states replace the border with `#EF4444` and apply a matching red focus aura.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`), `1px solid #E2E6EF`, and Level 1 elevation.
- Interactive cards feature a smooth transition: on hover, the border subtly warms toward `#FF6B2C` (at 30% opacity) and elevates to Level 2.

### Badges & Chips
- Status badges employ a pill shape (`9999px`), `label-sm` uppercase styling with `0.05em` letter spacing, and a padded footprint of `4px 10px`.
- Growth/Performance badge: 10% tint background of `#FF6B2C` with high-contrast `#FF6B2C` text.
- Neutral badge: `#F7F8FB` background with `#0A0E1A` text and `#E2E6EF` border.

### Lists & Key Metric Rows
- List items feature subtle bottom borders (`1px solid #E2E6EF`), generous vertical spacing (`space-md`), and integrated icon glyphs aligned to an obsidian circular container with orange micro-accents.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Geometric form with `4px` corner radius for checkboxes and circular form for radios. Unchecked state: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` against `#FFFFFF`. Checked state: solid `#FF6B2C` fill with white checkmark icon or radio dot.
